## Required report prompt

> ok now generate the report: The report (10–15 pages)

> Write it for a smart manager who has never seen your app. Use these sections, in this order. Put screenshots of your app wherever they help the reader see what you are describing.

> 1. Title + links: Project name, your name, the GitHub repo link, the link to the app itself if it is online, and one sentence that says what the app does.

> 2. Motivation: Who has this problem? How do they deal with it today, and what does that cost them in money, time, or frustration? Why should anyone care?

> 3. What is new here: Look around first. What already exists (apps, startups, ChatGPT itself)? What does your project do that those don't? Be honest.

> 4. The agents: What are the AI agents, what tools can each agent use, how are they organized, which models were used and why, and walk through one real run with screenshots.

> 5. Tokenomics: Measure input, cached input, and output tokens from real runs; calculate per-run, monthly, 100-user, and 10,000-user cost; explain cost controls and performance trade-offs.

> 6. Safety: Cover prompt injection, data leakage, malfunction, hallucination, wrong actions, loops, and overspending. Show at least one defense working with a screenshot or test.

> 7. Impact: Use numbers for money, time, quality, reach, or turnaround, explain the source, and prioritize real-user evidence.

> 8. Limits + next steps: Explain what does not work yet and what would be built next.
