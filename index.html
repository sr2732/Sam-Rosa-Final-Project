ABOUT

Sam
Rosa

Sam Rosa is a Balanced Body Method trained Pilates instructor with a background in athletics, including softball, basketball, and weightlifting. After multiple injuries and a connective tissue disorder diagnosis, she thought her body wasn't 'built' for high intensity movement. But after being introduced to Pilates through physical therapy, she rebuilt her stability and mobility and once again enjoys pain-free movement practice on and off the reformer!

Sam trained under renowned Maria Leone at Bodyline Studios in Beverly Hills, CA with additional mentorship and instruction by endurance athlete Colette van Dyk and '2025 Instructor of the Year' Miku Yoshida.

Sam has a diverse private client base in Los Angeles, with a focus on beginners, athletes, and individuals from non-traditional Pilates backgrounds. She worked as a Sales Associate & Front Desk Attendant at Pilates Addiction's flagship studio in West Hollywood, CA and has experience with membership sales, client retention, and studio operations.

CLASSICAL + CONTEMPORARY PHILOSOPHY

Classical +
Contemporary

Sam believes the role of instructors is to help clients safely achieve their movement goals. She respects classical fundamentals and postures as the core of her practice, while integrating props for modifications and advanced enhancements. Sam believes we should respect the Pilates tradition while also integrating modern science for an all-around, comprehensive practice.
